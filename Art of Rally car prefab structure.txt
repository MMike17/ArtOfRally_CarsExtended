Art of Rally car prefab structure

Body (contains 3D models as children / not the wheels)
Collider (MeshCollider with collider for the body)
Wing_Back (where you would put a wing)
Wing_Front (at intersection between front and top of front bumper)
Wheels
    WheelFL (center of each wheel)
    WheelFR (center of each wheel)
    WheelRL (center of each wheel)
    WheelRR (center of each wheel)
        WheelRR
            BrakeRotorRR (brake disc model)
            w(number of wheel) (wheel model)
        Caliper
            brake caliper (model)

    Lights
        Brakelights
            BrakeLight L (empty object just in front of breaklight)
            BrakeLight R (empty object just in front of breaklight)
        Headlights
            Headlights (pos of the light cone / slightly angled down in front of windshield above bonnet)
            AuxHeadlights [O]
                Light1 (broken version ?)
                    headlight_stick_left1 (copy of the light bulbs models)
                headlight_stick_left1 (light bulbs models)
                headlight_stick_right1 (light bulbs models)
            StockHeadlights
                headlights_stick_front (light bulbs models)
        Backfire
            Backfire L (pos where backfire effect spawns / up is forward)
        Nightlight (1/1.5 above the car)
    Particles
        Dust (where dust spawns / center of car slightly back)

        EngineSteam (where engine fires spawns)
